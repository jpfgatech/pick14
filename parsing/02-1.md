# Parsing: Decompose the value of a match (instructions/02-1.md)

## Goal

A match gives two distinct benefits:
1. **Direct**: the score-value of the captured cards lands in the score pile.
2. **Indirect**: the player gets to draw to n_hand+1 and play (discard) the worst
   card.  This improves future hand quality without any direct points.

We want to estimate each contribution separately via a randomised causal experiment.

---

## Experiment design

For each game, each player is independently designated a **candidate** (p=0.5).

### Interventions applied only to candidates

| Situation | Intervention (p=0.5) | Effect |
|-----------|----------------------|--------|
| Player takes a match | Unscore it — subtract the captured score-value from the player's final tally | Indirect benefit preserved; direct scoring removed |
| Player is forced to pass (no legal match) | Pseudo-match — dump 1 random hand card (out of game), draw 2, play 1 | Indirect benefit injected; no direct scoring |

### Counting per player per game

| Event | n_direct Δ | n_indirect Δ |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| Scored match (normal or candidate un-intervened) | +1 | +1 |
| Unscored match (candidate, direct removed) | 0 | +1 |
| Pseudo-match (candidate on forced pass) | 0 | +1 |
| Regular pass (no intervention) | 0 | 0 |

### Regression

OLS with two predictors:

```
score_adj = a + b1 × n_direct + b2 × n_indirect
```

Run in three forms matching instruction 02 (raw / raw-var / var):
- **Raw**: y = score_adj; x unchanged
- **Raw-var**: y = score_adj − game_avg; x unchanged
- **Var**: y = score_adj − game_avg; x = (n_direct − avg, n_indirect − avg)

Report coefficients **with standard errors** (from OLS covariance matrix).

---

## Pseudo-match mechanics

Normal pass sequence:
  PassMatch → PLAY (discard to pool) → Draw2 → next player

Pseudo-match replaces the PassMatch step:
  dump 1 random hand card (gone from game) → draw 2 → PLAY (choose from n+1) → next player

Net hand size and public-pool addition are identical to a normal pass.
The benefit is the *selection* opportunity: choose which of n_hand+1 cards to expose.

---

## Expected outcomes

- `b1 >> b2`: direct scoring dominates.  b1 ≈ average capture value per scored
  match (~4–8 pts depending on cards).
- `b2 > 0`: the draw+play opportunity has real value even without scoring.
- `b1 + b2 ≈ 6 pts` (matching the Var slope from instruction 02).
- Consistency across 2p / 3p / 4p: both b1 and b2 should be roughly stable.
